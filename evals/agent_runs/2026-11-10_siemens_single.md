# Deal-Prep Briefing: Siemens

## Company snapshot
- **Entity**: Siemens AG (siemens.com) [5].
- **Headquarters**: Berlin and Munich, Germany [5].
- **Leadership**: President and CEO Roland Busch [2].
- **Core Focus**: Industry, infrastructure, mobility, and healthcare [5].
- **Strategic Direction**: Deconsolidating and giving up its controlling stake in Siemens Healthineers through a planned spin-off [1, 2].

## Recent news
No recent news found (within the last 48 hours). The following older news from the last 30 days is available as background:

- **November 5–6, 2026 (Background)**: Parent company Siemens AG is proceeding with the spin-off and deconsolidation of Siemens Healthineers [1, 2].
- **Shareholder Vote & Deconsolidation (Background)**: Shareholders are scheduled to vote on the spin-off of Siemens Healthineers shares at an annual meeting in February [1]. The formal separation will take effect after entry into the German Commercial Register [2].
- **Rebranding & Carve-out (Background)**: Following the spin-off, Siemens Healthineers will start a three-year transition to rebrand as Healthineers, dropping "Siemens" from its name [1, 2, 3, 5], while its in vitro diagnostics (IVD) business will be carved out into a standalone entity named Olyrian Diagnostics [1, 4].
