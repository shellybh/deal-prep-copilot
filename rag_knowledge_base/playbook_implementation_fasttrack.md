# Sales Playbook: Selling Implementation Confidence with FastTrack 45

**Doc ID:** PB-002 | **Owner:** Sales Enablement (Grace Adeyemi) & Professional Services (VP PS, Robert Chen) | **Last Updated:** September 22, 2026 | **Classification:** Internal Only

## Why This Playbook Exists

In our 2026 Closed-Lost analysis, **"implementation risk / timeline concerns"** was the #1 loss reason in Enterprise deals. It was cited in **31% of losses**, ahead of price (24%). Buyers who have lived through a failed BI migration are afraid of a repeat. This playbook explains how to position our onboarding programs so they reduce perceived risk.

## FastTrack 45: Program Details

**The Guarantee:** NexaStream commits to production go-live of the agreed initial use case within **45 calendar days** of the kickoff meeting. If we miss this date for reasons attributable to NexaStream, the customer receives a **credit of 10% of first-year subscription fees**.

**2026 YTD Performance (through September 2026):**
- Enterprise implementations started: **47**
- Average time to go-live: **38 days**
- On-time rate: **94%** (44 of 47)
- Credits paid: **3** (total $61,400)

**Customer Prerequisites (must be in the Order Form):**
1. Named executive sponsor and project lead assigned before kickoff
2. Data source access credentials provided within **5 business days** of kickoff
3. Initial scope limited to **one primary use case** and **up to 10 data sources**
4. Customer SSO/IdP configuration completed by **day 15**
5. User acceptance testing (UAT) sign-off within **5 business days** of UAT start

If the customer misses a prerequisite, the guarantee clock pauses. The guarantee does not apply to phased multi-site rollouts unless a phase-specific milestone is defined, as was done for MeridianHealth.

## The 45-Day Timeline

| Phase | Days | Key Milestones |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Kickoff & Discovery** | 1–7 | Success criteria signed, source inventory, architecture review |
| **2. Connect & Model** | 8–20 | Flow connectors live, semantic layer modeled, SSO configured |
| **3. Build** | 21–32 | Pulse dashboards and alerts built, Sentinel policies applied |
| **4. Validate (UAT)** | 33–40 | Data reconciliation, user testing, performance tuning |
| **5. Launch** | 41–45 | Production cutover, admin training, hand-off to CSM |

## How to Position It (Talk Track)

> "We know analytics projects have a reputation for dragging on. That's why we put our money where our mouth is. FastTrack 45 guarantees go-live in 45 days or you get 10% of your first-year fees back. This year our average is 38 days, and we've hit the date 94% of the time."

## Discovery Questions to Uncover Implementation Fears

- "Tell me about your last data platform rollout. What went well and what didn't?"
- "Who on your team would own this project day to day?"
- "Is there a business deadline, such as a board meeting, audit, or peak season, that this must be live for?"
- "How many data sources are critical for the first use case?"

## Use the Mutual Action Plan (MAP)

For any Enterprise deal over **$150,000 ACV**, build a **Mutual Action Plan** with the customer that covers both the buying steps and the first 45 days of implementation. Deals with a signed MAP closed at a **2.1x higher rate** in H1 2026. The Vantage Logistics MAP had 11 milestones.
