# Sales Playbook: Objection Handling (Cost & Implementation)

**Doc ID:** PB-001 | **Owner:** Sales Enablement (Grace Adeyemi) | **Last Updated:** October 8, 2026 | **Classification:** Internal Only

## The LAER Framework

All NexaStream reps use **LAER** for objections:

1. **Listen:** Let the customer finish. Don't interrupt.
2. **Acknowledge:** Validate the concern ("That's a fair concern, and we hear it often").
3. **Explore:** Ask a clarifying question to find the real issue behind the objection.
4. **Respond:** Answer with a proof point, a number, or a customer story.

Rule of thumb: **Explore before you Respond.** In our 2026 call-recording analysis, reps who asked at least one clarifying question before answering an objection had a **27% higher stage-progression rate**.

## Objection 1: "NexaStream is too expensive."

**Explore:** "Too expensive compared to what: another vendor's quote, your current spend, or your budget?"

**Respond by scenario:**
- **Versus a usage-based competitor (CloudNova):** Show bill variance. "Our Professional tier is a fixed $4,800/month. CloudNova customers tell us their bills vary 30–45% month to month. What would a 40% spike do to your budget?"
- **Versus current spend:** Use the ROI calculator (Enablement Hub > Tools). Apex Financial cut analytics stack cost by **72%** and reached payback in **7 months**.
- **Versus budget:** Offer to start smaller. Begin on Professional and expand later, as Lumos Retail did when it moved from $86,400 to $198,000 ACV within six months.
- **Versus open source (QuantaLake):** Compare TCO, not license cost. Two engineers at about $180,000 each cost more than a NexaStream Enterprise subscription.

**Never:** Offer a discount in response to the first price objection. See `pricing_volume_discount_rules.md`. Discounts require a give-get.

## Objection 2: "Implementation will take too long."

**Explore:** "What happened on your last analytics implementation? What timeline does your business need?"

**Respond:**
- "Our Enterprise customers have gone live in an **average of 38 days in 2026**, under our **FastTrack 45** program. If we miss the 45-day go-live for reasons on our side, you receive a **10% credit on first-year subscription fees**."
- Point to proof: Apex Financial went live in 41 days, Vantage Logistics in 34 days, and MeridianHealth's Phase 1 in 29 days.
- Contrast: DataSphere cloud migrations average **6–9 months**.
- See `playbook_implementation_fasttrack.md` for full program details.

## Objection 3: "We don't have the internal resources."

**Explore:** "Which roles are you most worried about: data engineering, admin, or change management?"

**Respond:** FastTrack 45 includes a dedicated **Implementation Manager and Solutions Architect**. The customer typically needs only **0.5 FTE** of a data engineer during implementation. Over 300 pre-built connectors remove most custom pipeline work.

## Objection 4: "We're already committed to [CloudNova / our cloud credits]."

**Respond:** "NexaStream is available on **AWS Marketplace**, so your spend can count toward your existing AWS commitment."

## Objection 5: "AI tools like InsightForge are cheaper and faster."

**Respond:** "Lumen is now **included in Enterprise** at no extra cost. Unlike tools with per-query AI fees ($0.08/query at InsightForge), there are no overage surprises. Every answer shows its SQL, so you can trust it."

## Objection 6: "Now isn't the right time."

**Explore:** "What would need to change for this to become a priority?" Tie the deal to a compelling event: a competitor renewal date, a fiscal year-end, or a regulatory deadline. Mention the **Q4 Year-End Upgrade Offer** (expires December 31, 2026) only if it is real and relevant.

## Escalation

If an objection blocks a deal over **$250,000 ACV**, bring in your RVP and the Competitive Intelligence team within **48 hours**.
