**Doc ID:** PR-001 | **Owner:** Revenue Operations & Deal Desk (Hannah Brooks) | **Effective:** October 1, 2026 – December 31, 2026 | **Classification:** Confidential, Internal Only

## Overview

This guide sets list pricing for NexaStream's three core subscription tiers for **Q4 2026**. All prices are in USD, billed annually in advance unless noted. Monthly billing is available on **Starter only**, at a **15% premium**.

## Q4 2026 Changes vs. Q3 2026

1. **Starter price unchanged** at $1,200/month. Viewer seats increased from 15 to 25.
2. **Professional streaming limit raised** from 25,000 to 50,000 events/sec.
3. **Lumen is now included in Enterprise** (previously a $3,500/month add-on). Reps should use this against InsightForge.
4. **Enterprise storage overage reduced** from $21 to $19/TB/month.

## Q4 Promotions (Expire December 31, 2026)

- **Year-End Upgrade Offer:** Professional customers who upgrade to Enterprise before Dec 31 receive **3 months of Enterprise at Professional pricing**.
- **Competitive Displacement Credit:** Up to **$25,000 in services credit** for customers migrating from DataSphere, CloudNova, or QuantaLake. Requires proof of the competitor contract.

## Packaging Rules

- Enterprise minimum term is **12 months**. Multi-year (2–3 year) terms are strongly preferred.
- Do not split Enterprise features out to sell them on Professional. For example, PrivateLink is never sold à la carte.
- Nonprofit and education customers qualify for a standing **20% discount** on Starter and Professional (verification required).
- All discounts follow the approval matrix in `pricing_volume_discount_rules.md`.
- API limits and overage rates are defined in `pricing_api_usage_limits_2026.md`.
