# Volume Discounting & Approval Matrix (FY2026)

**Doc ID:** PR-003 | **Owner:** Deal Desk (Hannah Brooks) & Finance (CFO Office) | **Effective:** February 1, 2026 (FY2026) | **Classification:** Strictly Confidential. Never share with customers.

## Guiding Principles

1. **Discount for commitment, not for asking.** Every discount must be tied to a concrete give-get: term length, volume, upfront payment, a case study, or a reference.
2. **Protect list price integrity.** Never quote a discount in the first meeting.
3. **All discounts are calculated on the annual subscription fee only.** Professional Services, overages, and capacity packs are discounted separately (see below).

## Discount Approval Matrix

| Total Discount (off list ACV) | Approver | Typical Turnaround |
|---|---|---|
| 0% – 10% | Account Executive (self-approve) | Immediate |
| 10.1% – 20% | Regional Vice President (RVP) | 1 business day |
| 20.1% – 30% | Chief Revenue Officer (CRO) | 2 business days |
| > 30% | Deal Desk Committee + CFO | 5 business days (weekly Thursday review) |

Starter tier discounts are capped at **10%**, with no exceptions. Starter is designed for self-serve purchase.

## Standard Volume Discount Bands (Enterprise)

Volume discounts are based on **total annual contract value (ACV)** before discount:

| Annual Contract Value | Recommended Discount Band |
|---|---|
| $174,000 – $249,999 | 0% – 8% |
| $250,000 – $499,999 | 8% – 15% |
| $500,000 – $999,999 | 15% – 22% |
| $1,000,000+ | 22% – 28% (CRO approval) |

## Term-Length Incentives (Stackable)

- **2-year commitment:** +5% additional discount and a **price lock** for the full term
- **3-year commitment:** +8% additional discount, price lock, and a **5% cap on renewal uplift**
- **Annual upfront payment of a multi-year contract:** +3% additional

*Example:* A $300,000 ACV, 3-year deal could receive 10% (volume band) + 8% (term) = **18% total, which requires RVP approval**.

## Seat & Consumption Volume Rules

- **Additional Enterprise Creator seats** (above the 100 included):
  - 101–250 seats: $65/user/month (list)
  - 251–500 seats: $55/user/month
  - 501+ seats: $45/user/month
- **Compute credit pre-purchase:**
  - 10,000–49,999 credits/year: 10% off
  - 50,000–199,999 credits/year: 18% off
  - 200,000+ credits/year: 25% off (Deal Desk review)

## Professional Services Discounts

- PS packages may be discounted up to **25% with RVP approval**. Any discount beyond that needs approval from the VP of Professional Services.
- **FastTrack 45** is already included in Enterprise and has no cash value. Do not offer it as a "discount."

## Prohibited Practices

- No discounts above **40%** under any circumstances (FY2026 board policy).
- No "free months" outside approved promotions (e.g., the Q4 Year-End Upgrade Offer).
- No side letters or verbal commitments on future pricing.
- No uncapped renewal price protection beyond 3 years.
- Do not let the customer pay in more than **4 installments per year** without Finance approval.

## End-of-Quarter Guidance

The Q4 2026 close date is **December 31, 2026**. The Deal Desk Committee will hold extra review sessions on **December 17, 22, and 29**. Deals with discounts over 30% submitted after **December 22** are not guaranteed approval for Q4 booking.
